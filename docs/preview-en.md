## 4. Medium description (one paragraph)

```
Myriad Bloom is a dimension-focused content mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge. It does not simply add a
new world to explore — it builds a set of realms, each with its own sky, terrain, ecology and survival
pressure. The Frost Realm mirrors the overworld's landscape but freezes it in a permanent afternoon, where
hypothermia, thirst, blizzards and pockets of stopped time decide whether you make it back. The Candy World
trades danger for strangeness. Six further realms are in development: a lava-scorched land, a Chinese-styled
realm, the Moon, a post-blast wasteland, a single-island garden, and a violet realm of floating crystals.
```

## 5. Long description (listing page body)

```
**Myriad Bloom** is a multi-dimension content mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** running on **NeoForge**.

Most dimension mods give you one new world. Myriad Bloom is built the other way around: a set of realms,
each designed as a complete setting rather than a reskin. Every realm gets its own biomes, structures,
mobs, drops, ambient effects and survival mechanics, and each is entered differently.

### Playable today

**Frost Realm** (`myriadbloom:permafrost`)
The overworld's terrain, mirrored and frozen. Rivers, mountains and coastlines line up with the world you
left, but every biome is snowbound and the sun never leaves the afternoon.

- **Hypothermia** — exposure drains your warmth; heat sources and fur gear buy you time
- **Thirst** — a separate survival track, with snow water and sweet drinks restoring different amounts
- **Blizzards** — a random event that closes in your view, halves your movement speed and leaves fresh snow behind
- **Time stasis** — a random event that freezes block entities and random ticks in a radius
- **Ice Ocean** — a frozen-ocean biome with its own arena at the centre

**Candy World** (`myriadbloom:candy`)
Candy terrain, its own palette, and a very different set of things to worry about.

### The boss

At the heart of the Ice Ocean waits the **Glacial Conglomeration** — 350 health, heavy slams, a telegraphed
leap, and a drop table that opens a time-themed gear line: the Glacial Conglomeration leads to the
Prism of Time and the Hourglass of Time.

### Coming realms

Scorched Land (lava), Elmyia (Chinese-styled), The Moon (void and regolith), Ashfall (post-blast ruins),
Central Garden (one enormous island) and Eternity (violet, crystal-lit).

### Also included

- **19 advancements** across three tabs — Myriad Bloom, Realm of Frost, World of Candy
- **A built-in Bedrock model loader** — drop a `.geo.json` (and optional `.animation.json`) into the mod and
  the entity renderer picks it up, with no third-party model mod required
- **Debug commands** — `/myriadbloom summon_boss`, `build_arena`, `arena_info`, `cache_info`,
  `random_event start|roll|stop`

### Getting in

Build a 4×4 frame of snow blocks with a 2×2 pool of water in the middle, then throw a diamond into it.

### Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.x · Java 21 · install on both client and server
```
